Brightpath Solutions Pvt. Ltd.

Employee Handbook & HR Policy - Version 3.2 - Effective 1 April 2026 (sample document for RAG demo; fictional company)

1. Purpose and Scope

This handbook explains the rules, benefits and responsibilities that apply to all employees of Brightpath Solutions Pvt. Ltd. It covers full-time employees, part-time employees and interns unless a section says otherwise. Contractors are covered only by the Code of Conduct and IT Security sections.

The policy year runs from 1 April to 31 March. If anything in this handbook conflicts with your employment contract, the contract takes priority. HR reviews this handbook every year.

2. Working Hours and Attendance

Standard office hours are 9:00 am to 6:00 pm, Monday to Friday, including a one-hour lunch break. Employees may use the flexi-time window and start any time between 8:00 am and 10:30 am, as long as they complete 8 working hours per day.

Brightpath follows a hybrid model: employees work from the office at least 3 days per week. Teams decide their own office days with their manager. Attendance is recorded automatically through the access card; employees working remotely must mark attendance in the HRMS portal before 11:00 am.

Three or more late arrivals (after 10:30 am) in one month without manager approval count as half a day of paid leave.

3. Code of Conduct

Employees must treat colleagues, clients and partners with respect. Harassment, discrimination and bullying of any kind are not tolerated. Complaints about sexual harassment go to the Internal Committee (IC), which handles every case confidentially.

Employees must avoid conflicts of interest and must declare any outside employment or business to HR. Gifts from vendors worth more than Rs. 2,000 must be declined or reported.

Company information, client data and source code are confidential and may not be shared outside Brightpath, even after an employee leaves the company.

4. Paid Leave (Earned Leave)

Every full-time employee gets 24 days of paid leave per year. Paid leave accrues at 2 days per completed month of service. New joiners receive leave on a pro-rata basis from their joining date.

Leave must be applied for in the HRMS portal and approved by the reporting manager. Leave of more than 3 consecutive working days must be applied for at least 7 days in advance. Weekends and public holidays that fall inside a leave period are not counted as leave days.

Part-time employees get paid leave in proportion to their working hours. Interns get 1 day of paid leave per completed month of internship.

5. Carry-over and Encashment of Leave

Unused paid leave of up to 10 days can be carried over to the next policy year. Any unused paid leave above 10 days lapses on 31 March and cannot be recovered.

The total paid leave balance, including carried-over days, can never exceed 34 days at any time.

Leave encashment is allowed only when an employee leaves the company. At exit, up to 30 days of unused paid leave are paid out at the employee's basic salary rate. Sick leave cannot be carried over or encashed.

Public holidays: Brightpath observes 12 public holidays per year. The holiday list is published in the HRMS portal every March.

7. Travel and Expenses

Business travel must be pre-approved by the manager in the HRMS portal. Employees travel by economy class for flights under 6 hours. Hotel stays are booked through the company travel desk.

A daily allowance (per diem) of Rs. 1,500 within India and USD 60 abroad covers meals and local transport. Expense claims, with receipts, must be submitted within 30 days of the trip ending; claims submitted later are not reimbursed.

8. Work From Home

On remote days, employees must be reachable on Teams during core hours of 11:00 am to 4:00 pm. Employees receive an internet allowance of Rs. 1,500 per month, paid with the salary.

A one-time home-office setup allowance of Rs. 10,000 is available after confirmation of employment, for a chair, monitor or desk. Fully remote work for more than 2 weeks needs approval from the department head.

9. IT Equipment and Security

Every employee receives a company laptop. Laptops are refreshed every 3 years. A lost or stolen laptop must be reported to the IT helpdesk within 24 hours.

10. Grievances and Contacts

Employees should first raise concerns with their manager. If the issue is not resolved within 10 working days, it can be escalated to the HR Business Partner, and then to the Head of HR.
